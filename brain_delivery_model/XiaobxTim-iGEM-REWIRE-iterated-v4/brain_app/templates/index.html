A map for experiments,
not a clinical forecast.

This literature-informed mechanistic model links five modules and exposes assumptions for design comparison. Its normalized parameters are not fitted to patient data, a particular capsid, or a clinical administration protocol.

  • APOE3-like means C112 editing without C158 editing.
  • APOE2-like risk proxy tracks double editing at C112 and C158.
  • Off-target burden compresses uploaded candidate sites into an effective pool.
  • Every predicted trend requires wet-lab validation.
